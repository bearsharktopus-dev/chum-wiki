## Overview

Vysera is an injectable suppressant that switches off a powered person's abilities for roughly three to four hours per dose. It is given only through a federal program under NSRA supervision, with doses counted as strictly as firearms: participants sign for every dose, return empties and unused doses, and have them matched against lot numbers. Derek Taylor (Bloodhound II) enrolled in the trial in late October 2025 to suppress his nightly werewolf transformation, taking fourteen doses a week and wearing a biometric monitoring bracelet. Each injection leaves a small hard nodule that dissolves within about 48 hours.

In Book 15, Congress discussed using Vysera to depower inmates so they could be held in ordinary prisons instead of Daedalus.

## Mechanics

- **Duration:** a fixed window of about three hours (the published therapeutic duration), after which the power returns.
- **Delivery:** injection; Derek uses a wrist injector with two slots (Vysera and an anti-Vysera) to control his transformation.
- **Side effects (Derek):** fatigue, joint pain, emotional flatness; a firm injection-site nodule.
- **Effect on unpowered people:** none beyond the injection site.

??? warning "Book 19: Front Towards Enemy"

    **The Stinger.** Through late summer 2026, victims of neck stings in Philadelphia's river wards tested positive for Vysera. The attacker was a creature later named [Dufay](../characters/dufay.md), which delivers the drug through a needle on its tongue. Derek recognized the victims' nodules as ordinary Vysera injection lumps, and noted that no enrolled participant had ever been short a dose - so whoever was behind it had enough to waste (Ch. 282). Powered victims lost their abilities for about three hours, then recovered; the creature kept a copy of each power.

    **Source (reader-known).** Interlude LTN.3 establishes that Vysera, along with Hypeman, comes out of Mrs. Xenograft's "beefrog" pipeline for the Kingdom of Keys, and that Dufay was built in part from prepared cell lines of Mr. Nothing, whose own power switches others' powers off by touch. Sam Small independently infers that "Vysera is made out of Mr. Nothing" - grown from his cells - and that Dufay produces the drug because it is made from the same source (Ch. 292); that specific claim is her inference and has not been confirmed by any Kingdom character.

    **Public reporting.** Abigail Silverman's Boston Globe article "VYSERA & VIRTUE" (Interlude AS.1, September 28, 2026) reported that Vysera is given to about 600 people nationwide; that the NSRA buys it from **Calder Supply Group LLC**, a Delaware LLC incorporated in March 2024 with no listed employees, a Wilmington mail-service address, and no other contracts, awarded under emergency non-competitive authority with no manufacturing site or chemist named; that at least nine sting victims had been identified, four of them powered; and that two independent labs found DNA from an unidentified second human contributor in a nodule excised from one non-enrolled victim. The article concluded that the government either knows the drug's biological origin and will not say, or bought it without knowing. The same day, Councilman Jamal Davis refused under oath to name the source of information he had received on the question.

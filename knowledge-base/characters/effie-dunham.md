## Background

Euphemia Dunham trained at the Harvard Stem Cell Institute and did her residency at Massachusetts General Hospital. Her public record credits her with curing a kind of paralysis and restoring fertility to infertile patients, with results the field calls "non-replicable." She has worked with DAAS. Her publications stop in 2014, when she "transitioned to the private sector"; Mr. Preclude later dismisses her background as "a decade of experience doing boutique IVF for rich dipshits."

After twenty-three years of grant applications and ethics boards, and research she believes was locked away as "ethically ambiguous," she was brought to Mr. Antithesis by Chezki Espinosa, who describes her as a colleague who had done biomedical work for his side for years. Antithesis hired her as Mrs. Zygote (a name she chose herself), explicitly not as Mrs. Zenith's replacement, reporting to Mrs. Xenograft.

## Powers & Abilities

### PERKS Assessment

Not publicly known. Research sources in Book 19 describe her as a Brain-type superhuman.

### Abilities

- **Biological genius** - Stem cells, embryonic development, and chimeric organisms. Her clone eggs produced a fresh cohort of Mrs. Xenograft's beefrogs, and she co-created Dufay.
- **Field bioconstructs** - Carries blue fluid in a satchel (reading as blood to Sam's blood sense) that grows into living constructs within minutes: at Frankford Creek, five faceless pink humanoids with corrosive touch that dissolve on a timer ("people who were ten minutes old"). Kingdom crews also carry her wax-sealed clay "amphorae," which burst into three-legged turrets that fire human molars; the manifest reads "Return unused amphoras to Z."
- **Hands** - Lena says her hands "could thread a needle in a moving car."

### Limitations

- Mr. Antithesis judges her impossible to steer through incentives - "She responds to curiosity" - and lacking in operational discipline.
- Oblivious to danger and to other people's discomfort in the field.

## Personality

Effie talks constantly, at full volume and enthusiasm, to whoever is nearest, "with no modulation for context, rank, or the psychological comfort of her audience." She is sincere and without guile, unkempt (smudged glasses, a cardigan, scuffed flats), and entirely driven by what is interesting: "I don't care about legal or illegal. I care about _interesting_. Give me interesting and I'll give you miracles." Asked whether she has ever killed anyone with her powers, she answers, "_Duh_."

She treats Dufay with open pride - praising the den he chose, narrating his technique mid-fight, cooing at a discarded power-mass in a bag. Lena, who finds her exhausting, privately admits it is "genuinely more pleasant to work with Effie than not."

    **Role:** Supporting (Interlude TF.3)

    Chezki Espinosa recommends her to Mr. Antithesis, and she arrives in his waiting room having already talked Mr. Preclude into exhaustion.     In a Kingdom debrief, "Z" questions why the Kingdom used a loose cannon like Aaron McKinley. Mr. Preclude explains that chaos is good for black markets, insults her background, and tells her to prove the market value of what she and "X" have been working on, noting that "N" speaks well of her preliminary results.

??? warning "Book 19: Front Towards Enemy"

    **Role:** Major

    In Interlude LTN.3, she arrives at Lena's lab to examine a glowing mass Dufay has shed, pitches stable transgenic lineages, and is revealed to have supplied the clone eggs for Cohort D of the beefrogs. In a flashback she presents Project Bullfrog to Kingdom leadership in Manhattan: Dufay, grown from Mr. Nothing and Deathgirl cell lines, beefrog tissue, and donor engrams. Mr. Antithesis's "We'll let it run" gets a "Yes!" from her.

    At Frankford Creek (289) she rides with the Kingdom cleanup crew, talking to a discarded power-mass in a bag, and shouts Dufay's name. In LC.1 she grows five pink constructs to cover the crew's retreat ("the boys are ready, let's skedaddle!"). Lucy King's psychometric read of the site records her calling Dufay's den "a good choice," telling Preclude to bring clover, and being addressed as "Z." From those clues the Auditors deduce "Mrs. Zygote" and identify Dr. Euphemia Dunham through research (290). The Red Shocks later find her amphorae at a Kingdom powder site in Grays Ferry (CM.2), labeled for return to "Z."

## Background

Clara Parker is the lawyer retained by the Delaware Valley Defenders, working alongside Councilman Jamal Davis on the team's legal, oversight, and evidentiary problems. Sam describes her in Book 2 as commanding "a unique sort of respect" among a team of superheroes - one even Playback doesn't joke around with. She has experience with congressional testimony.

## Powers & Abilities

### PERKS Assessment

Not applicable. Clara is not known to have powers.

### Abilities

- **Evidence law** - Her recurring role is judging what can survive a courtroom: admissibility of drone footage (Book 2), what counts as a firsthand account, spoliation, chain of custody.
- **Legal strategy** - Handles SARA and regulatory fallout, witness protection logistics, and hearing preparation.
- **Protective procedure** - Structures conversations so that nothing is said which she would have to write down, and advises minors on how to protect themselves legally.

## Personality

Crisp, exacting, and composed. She wears glasses and pearl earrings, takes notes on a legal pad before anyone else has spoken, and lets her face "do absolutely no work at all." Bianca Agnelli sees "East Coast WASP rigidity" in her but calls her one of the sharpest legal minds she has met, and her composure cracks mainly around federal court matters and lawyers she distrusts, such as Gerald Caldwell.

Clara's protectiveness is procedural rather than warm, though she can be both. She tells Sam to photograph her bruises with a ruler in frame because "your body is the chain of custody," hands her a card marked "anytime," and in a crisis opens with "Are you safe? Right now, this minute?"

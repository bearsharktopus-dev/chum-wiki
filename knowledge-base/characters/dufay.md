# Dufay

> A seven-foot, frog-like artificial organism that stalks Philadelphia in the late summer of 2026, stinging powered people in the neck with a needle-tipped tongue - known to the public as "the Stinger" or "the South Philly Stinger." Victims lose their powers for about three hours and recover; Dufay keeps a copy.

## Background

Dufay first surfaces as a pattern of neck stings in North and South Philadelphia in late August 2026: a slow-clotting puncture, swelling, a hard subdermal nodule, and a tox screen reading Vysera. Derek Taylor tracks the creature to a nest in a collapsed riverfront warehouse and meets it face to face; Rogue Wave's field team is stung during a raid in Fishtown; and the fight-club fighter Moloch Horrible is stung outside a card. By Chapter 285 the press has named it "the South Philly Stinger."

The Kingdom of Keys built him. Under the name **Project Bullfrog**, Mrs. Zygote (Dr. Euphemia "Effie" Dunham) and Mrs. Xenograft (Dr. Lena Trinh-Norwood) grew Dufay from prepared cell lines of Mr. Nothing and Deathgirl, tissue from Mrs. Xenograft's "beefrogs," and behavioral scaffolding drawn from the engrams of eleven or twelve human donors, Lena among them. Presented to Kingdom leadership in Manhattan as a 280-pound, heavily sedated animal in a reinforced cage, he was approved for release with Mr. Antithesis's words: "We'll let it run."

In the field, a Kingdom cleanup crew under Mr. Preclude sterilizes his dens, feeds him, and retrieves the power-bearing masses he sheds. He was named on-page when Effie shouted "Dufay!" at him during the Frankford Creek confrontation.

## Powers & Abilities

### PERKS Assessment

Not publicly assessed.

### Abilities

- **Vysera sting** - A long, sausage-casing tongue ending in a thick black needle flanked by pincers. The sting delivers a Mr. Nothing-derived suppressant that switches off the victim's powers for about three hours - "same clock" as a therapeutic Vysera dose - and leaves an encapsulated, inert nodule.
- **Power copying** - Dufay keeps a copy of each power he takes; victims recover theirs. Maggie O'Brien: "he's not stealing powers. he's pirating them." Effie calls it "a loan... we keep a copy." Powers observed or inferred in him include Dead Drop's power-sense (inferred), Moloch Horrible's coral-like bone growths, a dockworker's glow, a cold power (frost vents, freezing touch, drawing water from air), and Lily Chen's "push."
- **Selective retention** - He sheds powers he does not want as living, culturable masses (the glow, and a "breathing" bag from an unidentified fifth victim), which the Kingdom collects.
- **Learning** - He improves with borrowed powers over time: after taking Lily's speed he repeatedly overshoots, then corrects within hours ("He gets good at things").
- **Physiology** - Roughly seven feet tall, with arms long enough that his knuckles reach his ankles, hand-like feet with gecko-adhesive pads, too many joints, fine black moth-like fuzz, a bony ridge along the spine, huge eyes that fold away when not in use, and an ear-to-ear mouth of pin-sized needle teeth. He climbs any surface "like the fence is just another floor" and makes a clicking croak from a large vocal sac.

### Limitations

- Kept docile in captivity with heavy doses of gabapentin.
- Stolen powers can give him away - after copying the glow he could not hunt without being seen, and went six days without stinging anyone until he shed it.
- Fed by his handlers on bee pollen, which leaves a retail trail.

## Personality

Every observer ends up describing him as an animal rather than a monster. He wears a cloak; Derek's unwanted thought is that "it wears a coat because it doesn't want to be looked at." He flees rather than fights when he can, grooms his fur like a cat, and practices his powers alone in the dark, failing and groaning. Lucy King, reading his night through psychometry: "Monsters do things at you... He's an animal."

He is also patient and selective. He chose his own den under the Betsy Ross interchange, watched the Kingdom van arrive before it arrived, and ignored his handler for two hours. Lena considers him "smart enough to be patient" and privately asks the question she cannot test: "how many people is Dufay?"

## Arc Appearances

??? warning "Book 19: Front Towards Enemy"

    **Role:** Major (antagonist)

    Stings begin in late August 2026. Derek finds the creature's nest near Graffiti Pier (DT.4) and backs away from a threat display; when he returns, the nest has been wire-brushed and bleached. Dufay stings Dead Drop during a Rogue Wave grab at a Kingdom-guarded Fishtown site (DC.4) - she reads Mr. Nothing and "three or four other people" inside him - and later stings Moloch Horrible outside a fight-club card, where Sam grabs his ankle and is peeled off "no anger in it at all." The public names him the South Philly Stinger; he glows for a week after copying a dockworker's power, then sheds it.

    Interlude LTN.3 reveals his origin as the Kingdom's Project Bullfrog and the leadership decision to "let it run." At Frankford Creek (289), the joint Auditors-Tacony Titans recon runs into his cleanup crew; he frostbites Mudslide's arm and is named by Effie. In LC.1 he watches Lily from under an overpass, stands in front of her, stings her palm, and leaves at her speed. Moonshot tracks him to a warehouse roof off Aramingo, where he eats bee pollen left by his handlers.

    After the DVD handover (291), Rampart is moved out of Philadelphia to keep his power out of Dufay's reach. Dufay is filmed crossing the underside of the Ben Franklin Bridge into New Jersey during the Red Shocks' rooftop rally and stops stinging for days. On Monday, September 28, Councilman Davis confirms under oath that an animal is hunting powered residents. Sam concludes that Dufay produces Vysera and is "being milked."
